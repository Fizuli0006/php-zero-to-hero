# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive learning community.

Expected behavior:
- Be respectful and patient with beginners.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Accept corrections gracefully.
- Focus on the work, not the person.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe and constructive.
